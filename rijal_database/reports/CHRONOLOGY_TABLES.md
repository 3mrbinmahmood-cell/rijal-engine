# Provisional Hijri death-year counts

Death years in Hijri chronology. Unlabelled complete years in Arabic death statements are provisionally treated as Hijri, flagged separately. Short years, approximate dates, conflicts, Gregorian-only dates and missing dates are excluded. Grouping uses extracted name labels plus matching death year; groups are not verified distinct people. Scope is all candidate biographies in the supplied collection, not only hadith narrators. No semantic identity reconciliation has been completed.

Verified distinct-person total: not yet established.
