# Reader V0.6.7

Apply `annotate_grades.py` to the V0.6.6 reader ZIP and `sample library v 6.zip`, then overlay `reader.js` and `index.html` from this folder. The annotation only covers a page with exactly one numbered report and an explicit grade phrase. Footnote phrases more than 160 characters into a footnote are ignored to avoid assigning another report's comparative grade.

The ruling panel appears before text. It retains separate main-text and footnote phrases and flags conflicting statements. The hide toggle conceals weak or conflicting pages until the reader chooses to reveal them. “الصحيح التالي” moves to the next page in the current book with a single explicit ṣaḥīḥ status. These phrases are source quotations, not an automatic explanation of the weakness or a verified identification of ambiguous narrators.
